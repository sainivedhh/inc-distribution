## 2. Hard Cases Verification Artifacts

The 7 mandatory hard cases are automated in `tests/hard_cases/`:
- **Hard Case 1 (Timeout Retry)**: `tests/hard_cases/test_hard_case_1_timeout_retry.py` (Proves zero duplicate posts on retry with identical idempotency key)
- **Hard Case 2 (Error Codes & Rate Limits)**: `tests/hard_cases/test_hard_case_2_errors.py` (Proves 429 reset time backoff, `CONNECTION_AUTH_EXPIRED`, `PLATFORM_CONTENT_REJECTED`)
- **Hard Case 3 (Spec Violations)**: `tests/hard_cases/test_hard_case_3_spec_violations.py` (Proves field-level reasons on over-long titles and unsupported media types)
- **Hard Case 4 (Rule Engine Enforcement)**: `tests/hard_cases/test_hard_case_4_rule_violations.py` (Proves frequency cap windows and self-promotion bans)
- **Hard Case 5 (Schema Drift & Demotion)**: `tests/hard_cases/test_hard_case_5_schema_drift.py` (Proves API schema drift triggers loud failure and immediate demotion to L0)
- **Hard Case 6 (CSV Duplicates, SSRF & Partner Revocation)**: `tests/hard_cases/test_hard_case_6_csv_and_revocation.py` (Proves duplicate deduplication, private IP SSRF isolation, and partner connection revocation)
- **Hard Case 7 (i18n & AI Disclosure)**: `tests/hard_cases/test_hard_case_7_i18n_and_disclosure.py` (Proves safe UTF-8 CJK/emoji word-boundary truncation and auto-injected AI disclosure tags)
